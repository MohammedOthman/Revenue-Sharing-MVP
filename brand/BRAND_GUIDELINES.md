## 01 · Brand

### The operating system for revenue shared between companies.

Reven exists because companies don't grow alone anymore — they grow with
partners. The brand has to feel as ordered and trustworthy as the system it
describes: **institutional, not playful.**

**The Reven belief**
> Partnerships aren't a relationship problem. **They're a revenue problem.**

**The three fixed lines** — *These three lines are fixed. Do not paraphrase them
in decks, decks, or product copy — quote them exactly.*

| Field | Line |
|---|---|
| **Category** | The Partner Revenue Operating System |
| **Positioning line** | The operating system for revenue shared between companies. |
| **Tagline** | Turning partnerships from a cost center into a profit center. |

**Voice pillars:** `Confident` · `Declarative` · `Literal` · `No hedging`

## 02 · Voice & Tone

### Confident, declarative, literal. Never clever for its own sake.

| Principle | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Flip the frame** | State the problem as a before/after contrast: "X get treated as Y, because Z" · THEN vs NOW. | |
| **One payoff per line** | Exactly one clause per headline is colored Accent Blue. | Never more, never zero. |
| **Literal names** | Section titles describe, they never tease. | "The Cost Center Trap," not a pun. |
| **No hedging** | Cut qualifiers — "maybe," "sort of," "we think." | State the claim. |

**Avoid** *(the anti-pattern):*
> "We're excited to help you streamline some of your partnership challenges, if
> that would be useful."

**Reven voice** *(the target):*
> "Not another PRM. The control system for partner-led revenue execution."

## 03 · Logo — The Mark

### One wordmark. One accent chip. No variations.

| Element | Specification |
|---|---|
| **The wordmark** | Sora, weight 700/800. Deep navy `#0F1629`. Rounded geometric terminals — never a substitute typeface. |
| **The accent chip** | Two-tone Accent Blue block breaking the R's counter and the foot of the n. The only permitted color break in the mark. |
| **The period** | In the lockup "Reven." the trailing period always renders in Accent Blue `#2C5BFF`. |

**Supplied master file — source of truth:** Construction and proportion
reference. Redraw or trace only from this file — never from memory or a
screenshot. *(See [`assets/logo/`](assets/logo/) and slide 3 of the master PDF.)*

**Rule:** There is exactly one wordmark. No condensed cut, no icon-only mark, no
alternate lockups.

## 04 · Logo — System

### Six ways to use it. Never a seventh.

| # | Use | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Clearspace** | Clearspace on every side ≥ the accent chip's height. |
| 2 | **Minimum size** | 120px on screen, 25mm in print. Below this the chip stops reading. |
| 3 | **Reversed / on dark** | Navy or photographic backgrounds only — never navy at reduced opacity. |
| 4 | **Monochrome / single color** | One-ink contexts — engraving, thermal print, single-spot merch. The chip stays tonal, never disappears. |
| 5 | **On photography** | Always add a navy scrim (≥40% opacity) behind the mark for legibility. |
| 6 | **Co-branding lockup** | Divider at cap-height, 20% ink. Reven never resizes to match — align by optical center. (`Reven │ PARTNER`) |

---

## 05 · Logo — Misuse

### Five ways to break it.

| ✗ | Misuse | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Wrong color** | Never recolor the wordmark — it is Ink or reversed white, always. |
| 2 | **Chip removed** | The accent chip and blue period are mandatory, not optional. |
| 3 | **Added effects** | No outlines, glows, bevels or drop shadows. |
| 4 | **Distorted** | Never stretch, squash or rotate the mark. |
| 5 | **Busy backdrop** | Give it clearspace on a calm surface, never a clashing one. |

## 06 · Color

### One saturated hue. Navy, cream and gray do the rest.

**Primary**

| Swatch | Hex | Name | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⬛ | `#0F1629` | **Ink** | Primary text & logo |
| ⬜ | `#FBFAF7` | **Canvas** | Default bg, never pure white |
| ⬜ | `#FFFFFF` | **Card White** | Elevated surfaces on canvas |
| 🟦 | `#2C5BFF` | **Accent Blue** | The only saturated hue — signal |
| 🟦 | `#1E47E0` | **Accent Deep** | Gradient partner for Accent Blue |
| 🟦 | `#8DA4FF` / `#AEC5FF` | **Accent Pale** | Headline highlights on dark panels |

**Muted / supporting**

| Hex | Role |
|---|---|
| `#5B6172` | Body copy |
| `#6B7180` | Captions |
| `#8A8F9C` | Tertiary / mono labels |
| `#9AA3B5` | Micro-labels |

**Status — use sparingly, never as a primary color**

| Hex | Role |
|---|---|
| `#C98A1B` | Caution / measure |
| `#C0392B` | Alert / dispute |
| `#1F8A5B` | Positive / live |

**Rule:** Blue is the only saturated hue in the system. Everything else is navy,
cream, or gray.

## 07 · Typography

### Three typefaces, three jobs. Never mixed.

| Role | Typeface | Weights | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Display** | **Sora** | 700, 800 | Headlines, stat numbers, nameplates only. Tracking -0.02 to -0.035em. |
| **Body** | **Manrope** | 400, 500, 600, 700 | All reading copy, pill labels, quoted lines. Regular tracking. |
| **Label** | **IBM Plex Mono** | 400, 500, 600 | Every eyebrow, index and footer meta. Always uppercase, always tracked 0.08–0.24em. Never body copy. |

- **Display** sample: *"Run partnerships like a predictable revenue line."*
- **Body** sample: *"Reven is the operating system for revenue shared between companies."*
- **Label** sample: `PARTNER REVENUE OS · INVESTOR PITCH`

## 08 · Layout Anatomy

### Every slide follows the same skeleton.

**Margins**

| Margin | Value |
|---|---|
| Top | 78–84px |
| Bottom | 104–112px |
| Side | 104px |

**The skeleton (top → bottom)**

1. **Header** — rule + eyebrow · `"NN · SECTION NAME"`
2. **Headline** — max-width · `text-wrap: balance` · one accent clause
3. **Primary content** — 55–60% of the body width
4. **Supporting panel / screenshot** — 40–45% of the body width
5. **Footer** — wordmark + index · 46px inset

**Principles**

| Principle | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Same margins everywhere** | 104px sides, ~80px top, ~108px bottom, on every slide without exception. |
| **Eyebrow always precedes headline** | The rule + mono index is never skipped, never replaced by an icon. |
| **Body is asymmetric** | Never an even 50/50 split — one side always leads. |
| **Footer repeats identically** | Wordmark left, 2-digit index right, on every single slide. |

## 09 · Components

### The building blocks, reused everywhere.

| Component | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Pill — default / selected** | Default = white pill, ink label (e.g. `Manage`). Selected = Accent Blue fill, white label (e.g. `Settle`). |
| **Dark contrast panel** | Deep-navy (`#0F1629`) panel carrying the key quote or stat, e.g. *"Partnerships aren't a relationship problem."* |
| **Stat card** | Mono eyebrow label + oversized Sora figure, e.g. `PARTNER'S CUT` → **12%**. |
| **Step chips** | Numbered sequence `01` (ink) · `02` (deep/violet) · `03` (Accent Blue), the last chip active/glowing. |
| **Comparison / gap bar** | Two stacked bars: `PRM` = short gray bar; `Reven` = full Accent Blue bar. Visualizes the gap. |
| **Browser chrome** | Neutral window frame (red/yellow/green traffic-light dots) that frames a product screenshot. |
| **CTA / callout bar** | Full-width Accent Blue bar, white Sora label left (e.g. *"Owns the whole journey"*), mono tag right (`END TO END`). |

## 10 · Motif

### A quiet constellation, never decoration for its own sake.

The node-and-flow constellation.

| Property | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Stroke** | 1–1.4px, thin and quiet. |
| **Opacity** | 0.15–0.55 — atmosphere, not artwork. |
| **Color** | Accent Blue only — never navy or gray. |
| **Where** | Dark-navy or gradient-tinted slides only — never on flat cream. |
| **Emphasis node** | One node may glow to mark a pivotal step. Use sparingly — at most once per slide. |

## 11 · Backgrounds

### Exactly three surfaces. Nothing else.

| Surface | Value | Use | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Flat Canvas** | `#FBFAF7` | The default. | ~80% of slides |
| **Radial Wash** | `#F0EAFF → #FBFAF7` | Cover and 1–2 transitions. | ~15% |
| **Deep Navy** | `#0F1629` (or `#1F1740 → #0F1629`) | Gravity slides for emphasis. | ~5% (2–3 slides) |

## 12 · In Use

### The system, assembled.

An example on-brand slide (`01 · THE COST CENTER TRAP`):

> **Partnerships get treated as a cost center, because no one can prove the
> revenue they _create._**
>
> It's hard to measure, hard to verify, and hard to connect to revenue actually
> collected.
>
> *(Dark contrast panel — `WHEN FINANCE ASKS`)* "Which partner created which
> revenue, and how much do we owe them?" → **● Usually unclear.**

**Four elements, every slide**

| # | Element | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Eyebrow + rule** | Mono index and section name, always paired with the short accent rule. |
| 2 | **One accent clause** | Exactly one phrase in the headline colored Accent Blue — the payoff word. |
| 3 | **Dark contrast panel** | The one moment of visual weight per slide — the key quote or stat. |
| 4 | **Footer — wordmark + index** | Repeats identically on every slide: wordmark left, 2-digit index right. |
