# Trend 09 — Management teams want measurable commercial outcomes rather than partnership announcements

| | |
|---|---|
| **Class** | Market-making — defines buyer psychology, but available to any competitor |
| **Use it for** | The **narrative and the metric to own**. Not for differentiation |
| **Dated clock** | None — but the governance change is permanent |
| **One line** | Intermediary management was the last major commercial function without a measurement standard, and it is now being held to one with no system of record to meet it |

---

## The claim

Executives no longer accept a logo, an announcement or a long relationship as evidence that a partnership is working. They want a number.

---

## Rationale — why it is true

**The correlation is documented.** Data-driven partner programmes show roughly **48% higher partner-influenced revenue growth** than unmeasured ones (MED).

**The deeper driver is sector-general.** Intermediary relationships were historically defended by *narrative* — the logo, the press release, "strategic alignment," the decade-long relationship — **because they could not be defended by numbers.** Once the payments become material, narrative defence stops being accepted.

Note that this applies identically to a channel chief defending a partner budget, a distribution head defending a broker network, and a franchise director defending a master-franchise agreement. The vocabulary differs; the exposure does not.

---

## Why now

The **2023–25 efficiency reset permanently changed how commercial functions are governed.** Every revenue motion now survives a quarterly review that did not exist in 2021.

And intermediary management was **the last major commercial function without a measurement standard**:

| Function | Its accepted standard |
|---|---|
| Sales | Pipeline, quota attainment |
| Marketing | Attribution, pipeline contribution |
| Customer success | Net revenue retention |
| **Distribution / partnerships / agency / franchise** | **Logos and gut feel** |

> In 2026 that function is being held to the same standard as the others — **and there is no accepted system of record with which to meet it.** That vacuum is the category, and it is a vacuum in every sector at once.

---

## Strategic implication

This trend defines **buyer psychology**, and it travels across sectors unchanged. The pitch is not *"manage partners better."* It is:

> **"Be able to answer the question you are about to be asked."**

The product's job is to convert a commercial function from unmeasurable to **defensible**.

**1. The economic buyer is whoever gets embarrassed by the question.** That is the commercial owner first (who needs the number to defend a budget) and the CFO second (who needs it to authorize the payment). **Both buy the same artifact — the evidence pack — for different fears.** This is why land-with-commercial / expand-to-finance works: it is not a compromise, it is the same product sold twice to two people with different exposures.

**2. Own a metric, not just a workflow.** Categories are won by whoever defines the number the market manages to — Salesforce defined pipeline, Gainsight defined NRR, Crossbeam defined overlap. Reven's candidate:

> **Trusted revenue-share flow** — intermediated revenue that has passed attribution, evidence and both-sides reconciliation.

That is **sector-neutral by construction** and far harder for an incumbent to copy than a feature. Publishing a defensible definition is a category-creation move available *now*, at near-zero cost.

---

## Operational implication

- **Make the evidence pack — not the dashboard — the primary deliverable.** The demo is *"here is what you send to finance,"* not *"here is your funnel."* **Dashboards are how this product gets commoditized.**
- **Instrument the Phase-1 exit gate literally:** 100+ real claims through the ledger; 3–5 design partners with a **finance-accepted** evidence pack; weekly active usage; time-to-first-claim under 14 days; and one CFO sentence — *"these numbers reconcile."*
- **Capture a before/after leakage number in every pilot** — duplicates caught, mis-attributions corrected, adjudication hours saved. **Underwrite the conservative 3–8% payout-error band** (Gartner-cited, MED), not the aggressive 10–30%. *A claim you beat is worth more than a claim you defend.*
- **Publish a KSA Revenue-Share Benchmark from anonymized pilot data, segmented by sector.** Nearly free once the ledger exists — and multi-sector data makes it a far stronger category-definition asset than a technology-only one, because **nobody else can produce it.**

---

## What would falsify it

If management wants measurement but **accepts a self-reported number from the counterparty**, the demand is for a report rather than a record, and the price ceiling collapses to reporting-tool economics.

**The tell:** does anyone independently *check* the number today? If the answer is no, and nobody is uncomfortable about that, the urgency is not there yet.
